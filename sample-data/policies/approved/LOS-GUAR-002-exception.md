# Approved Limited Guaranty Exception

A guaranty may be limited to a stated amount where the guarantor pledges cash collateral equal to twenty-five percent of the loan amount, held in a Bank-controlled account for the term of the loan. The limited guaranty must still cover every twenty-percent owner; the exception changes the amount, not the parties.

## Use when

Use for borrowers with institutional or employee-ownership structures where an unlimited guaranty from every owner is impractical and the cash collateral can be funded at closing.

## Approval note

Loan Documentation and Credit Risk must both approve this exception. A limited guaranty without cash collateral, or the omission of an owner, is outside policy and outside this exception.
