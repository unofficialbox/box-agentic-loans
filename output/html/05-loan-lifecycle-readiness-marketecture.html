AI-Assisted Loan Origination

Loan Origination (LOS)

↔
One end-to-end lifecycle. Persistent platform responsibilities.
Every platform contributes across the loan lifecycle; governed Apex actions are the only path between them.
01

Application Intake

Capture the application package, establish ownership, and create the governed loan file.

Intake begins when loan metadata is applied in Box; Box Automate starts the governed process.

02

Classification & Enrichment

Extract loan terms, apply metadata, and connect borrower context.

Extracted amount, rate, term, collateral value, LTV, and DSCR are accepted by a loan officer before the loan record is updated.

03

Policy & Covenant Analysis

Compare the application to the credit policy library and prior executed agreements, explain exceptions, and route domain findings.

Cited departures are mapped to Credit Risk, Collateral, Compliance, Loan Documentation, or another named domain expert.

04

Cross-Source Credit Validation

Reconcile borrower-stated terms with the appraisal, financial statements, and Salesforce loan context.

Box content and Salesforce context are compared without changing system authority.

05

Human Decision Gate (Credit Committee)

Credit Risk, Collateral, Compliance, and the Credit Committee assess material findings.

Named reviewers approve, reject, or request changes before any commitment letter, signature, or funding action.

06

Approved Closing & Servicing

Issue the commitment letter, close the loan, and manage covenants, maturities, and governed records.

Approved workflows update loan status, preserve decision evidence, and set covenant review and maturity dates.

Persistent platform responsibilities across every lifecycle stage

Salesforce Agentforce

Borrower and credit operating context: accounts, opportunities, loan records, loan officers, credit approvals, and covenants.

Standard Salesforce REST upserts the LOS loan record by external ID; Agentforce uses the same governed loan context, and accepted terms reach the record only through the human-confirmed apply action.
