Loan lifecycle journey

1. Apply

Start with a complete package

Capture the application, financials, appraisal, and insurance through a consistent, policy-aware entry point.

2. Underwrite

Turn documents into evidence

Extract loan terms, compare them to credit policy and precedent, and surface exceptions with citations.

3. Decide

Engage the right experts

Route credit risk, collateral, compliance, and documentation questions to named owners with cited source context.

4. Close

Commit and sign securely

Preserve decision rights, signature authority, versions, and the final executed loan package.

5. Service

Manage what was promised

Track covenants, maturities, insurance renewals, and portfolio risk over time.
